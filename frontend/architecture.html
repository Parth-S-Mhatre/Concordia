SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How the platform fits together.

Four layers: static clients talk to a FastAPI service layer, background jobs do the heavy lifting in batches, and Firestore persists every source, record, entity, and job with full provenance. Full detail in ARCHITECTURE.md.
